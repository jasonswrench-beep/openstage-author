# Notes that follow a video

A short example of timing that follows a video or an audio clip (format 0.23.0), made with the editor itself.

| Slide | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| A video with notes | A 20 second test video (a pattern with a clock that counts the seconds) and four notes. One is there from the start. One arrives at 3 seconds and goes at 9. One arrives at 10 seconds and stays until the end. One arrives at 15 seconds. Skip ahead or go back, and what shows is what belongs to that moment. |
| An audio clip with notes | A 15 second tone and two notes, at 2 seconds (gone at 7) and at 8 seconds. |

Try it in the published course: **Pause timing** pauses the video or audio too, and **Resume timing** plays it again. **Show everything now** shows every note and leaves the video alone. Take the video file out of
`assets/video` and the notes all show, because nothing should be hidden because a clip did not play.

The video and the tone were made for this example with ffmpeg, from test signals, so they need no credit. The video has captions and a transcript, and the tone has a transcript.
The two Next and Back buttons use icons from Lucide (ISC licence), so the credits page is on.
