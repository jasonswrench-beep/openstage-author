# Giving feedback: the parts

A short example made with the editor itself, to show the parts added in format 0.22.0. It has no purpose beyond showing them and checking they keep working.

| Slide | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Welcome | The ready-made Welcome slide, with its Begin button already wired to go to the next slide. |
| Objectives | The ready-made Objectives slide, with Back and Next. |
| Three ways to open | Three decorative icons (the words under each say what it means), and buttons in three looks: Soft (Back), Outline fully rounded with an icon (Replay this slide), and Filled fully rounded with an icon after the words (Next). |
| A conversation | The ready-made Scenario slide, made into a short story. Maya and Alex are the project's cast, and their pictures were drawn by the avatar maker (`assets/images/maya.svg` and `alex.svg`). Choices change a "trust" number that a meter shows. |
| Knowledge check | The ready-made Knowledge check slide, with its question and feedback written. |
| Summary | The ready-made Summary slide, with Back. |

The icons come from Lucide (ISC licence), so the credits page is on; a published course carries the licence as `licenses/lucide.txt`.
Nothing here has narration. To make it, open the project in the editor and use the Narration button.
