Character packs that came with OpenStage Author.

Each folder here is a pack: pictures of people and a pack.json that describes them. In the editor, open The cast and choose
"Choose from the library" to pick someone. Packs you add yourself are kept in your own data folder, not here (the same dialog has
a button that shows that folder, and a button that adds a pack from a zip file).

Do not change these folders. Installing OpenStage Author again puts them back as they were.
How a pack is made, and how to make one of your own: docs/character-packs.md.
