Packs that came with OpenStage Author: characters (people in many poses) and backgrounds (pictures to put behind a slide).

Each folder here is a pack: pictures and a pack.json that describes them. In the editor, open Characters and choose
"Choose from the library" to pick a person, or select a slide and use "Background picture..." to pick a background. Packs you add yourself are kept in your own data folder, not here (the same dialog has
a button that shows that folder, and a button that adds a pack from a zip file).

Do not change these folders. Installing OpenStage Author again puts them back as they were.
How a pack is made, and how to make one of your own: docs/character-packs.md.
